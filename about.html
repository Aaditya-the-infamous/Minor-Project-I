About Quizzly

Quizzly is a small interactive quiz application built to practice front-end fundamentals — structuring pages, styling a responsive interface, and wiring up logic with plain JavaScript. Pick a category, answer against the clock, and get an instant, itemized breakdown of what you got right.

  • 01Pick a category : General Knowledge, Science, Sports, Technology, or History.
  • 02Answer against the timer , each question gives you 10 seconds; running out counts as incorrect.
  • 03See your score instantly, a summary shows your percentage and a question-by-question review.
  • 04Try again anytime, restart a category or explore a different one.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no backend, just the fundamentals. This site is a true multi-page application: each section (Home, Categories, About, Contact, Quiz, Result) is its own HTML file linked together, sharing one stylesheet and one question-bank script.
